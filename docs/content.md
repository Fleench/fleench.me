# Site Content (content.md)

The site functions as a personal digital garden, featuring several core content types:

- **Index/Landing**: Main entry point at `index.md`.
- **Blog**: Long-form entries located in `src/blogs/`.
- **Notes**: Rapid, timestamped thoughts located in `src/notes/` (organized by date folders).
- **Static Pages**: Informational pages including `about.md`, `ai.md`, `changelog.md`, `todo.md`, and `now.md`.
- **Media**: Assets and metadata are housed in `src/media/`.

All content is authored in Markdown, allowing for rapid iteration and version control.
