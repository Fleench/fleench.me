Not your average retreat: Seek26(1/?)

Energy was high as I walked into my university's Newman House. For those who don't know, a Newman Center/House is a building home to the Catholic Ministry of a university in the US. While I was at Sunday evening mass the Focus Missionaries spent the time setting up for what they called the seek party. I had no idea what to expect.

Most of you are like me and have no idea what a seek party is. If that's you a seek party is a party my campus's Catholic ministry holds once a year to promote the upcoming Seek Conference hosted by the Fellowship of Catholic University Students (FOCUS). As a first-year student, I had no idea what the SEEK conference was. As I was about to find out, Seek is not just a small gathering. Seek is an annual conference held by FOCUS for Catholics in the US featuring talks, adoration, and a chance to grow.

When I first found out, I jumped at the chance to sign up. I saw it as just an extremely large retreat where I can learn a lot. I loved every retreat I went on in high school. Little did I know Seek would fill me with the desire to change my life.

Through this mini-series on seek26 I will share the work I had to do to get there, the most impactful things I learned, the great experiences I had, and how I plan to change my life.

Glenn Sutherland
