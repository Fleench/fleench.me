# AI Policies
I have a love hate relationship with AI. As a technology I think it is so cool but as a content creator I don't like the way it is used to replace content. That is why I aam creating this page. A way to share how AI will be used in the creation of my website.

To begin I do not ever plant to have any if my content be generated by AI. The one exception is the page, but I will explain more later. 

As for where I will be using AI it will be in primarily in code generation for my static site generator or to preform cleanup tasks.

Now for using AI while not yet setup I will be hosting an OpenClaw instance. I have already planned to name it HoltBot and base its personality off of Captain Holt from Brooklyn 99. In doing so I believe OpenClaw is an amazing technology. That is why at the bottom of this page I'm creating a spot for HoltBot to post updates on what he is doing or other content. 

Now to make HoltBot not so hidden I am also going to allow you guys to ask HoltBot questions. Though to keep it secure I will not be opening a public channel directly to HoltBot. Instead, email all questions to webmaster@flench.me and I will vet them and feed them to HoltBot where replies will appear in his content section.

The last thing I'd like to bring up is how often HoltBot will be used. My plan is to allow HoltBot to run any tasks asked of it at most 4 times a day. This is to save on tokens and to prevent him from always running. 

As always if you have any questions on this page just drop a webmention or email webmaster@flench.me.
