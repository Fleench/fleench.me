While at the seek party one of things talked about was the price of the trip. It was estimated to be over $700. That included the cost of the event pass and the hotel room. I thought How is a college student supposed to afford this? But as it turns out it’s not difficult.

In focus the missionaries fundraise their salaries. So, we employed the same technique they did. We asked the people we knew to support us. It works like this. We brainstorm around 50 names of people we know. These are people such as youth pastors, family members, clergy, doctors, or anyone. Then we pick 30 of those people and we write them a fundraising letter. We tell them about seek and ask them to support us on their mission. Upon doing this you can raise most of the trip. I only had to cover around $150 out of pocket. That is a lot easier for a college student.

I found out I only at $150 I had to cover after I got home for Christmas break. It was less than a month until seek26 I could not wait. Though I couldn’t do much more to prepare until it was about a week out. By that point, the packing list was out. In general, there is not much you need. A basic list is provided below.

- Clothes for 5 days
- Modest PJs
- Nice church clothes
- A bible
- A journal for notes
- A water bottle
- Toiletries
- Pens

Those were some items on the official list my missionaries gave out. However, I also recommend a backpack of some sort. While they do give a tote bag or drawstring back at check in you may accidentally confuse it with someone else. I also recommend ear plugs for the concert they have on day 4.

The last thing you need is the seek app on your phone. This app is the official app for the conference holding the schedule and listing out the talks. It also used to send announcements of things happening. You could get by without it, but it would make things harder.

With the logistics out of the way we can enjoy the conference. I’ll explore the mission pathways in my next post.
